Scope of Coverage

  • Photography — candid and posed coverage of performances, vendors, the business workshop, crowds, and sponsor/partner signage each day.
  • Videography — multi-angle video of performances, the street jams, and the exhibition, shot for both the daily highlights and the full recap edit.
  • Attendee & performer interviews — short on-camera interviews conducted across all three days, to be woven into the full festival video rather than published on their own.
  • Same-week turnaround on daily highlight edits, so each day's video is ready to post while the festival is still running.
